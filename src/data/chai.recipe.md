title: Chai
---

## Ingredients

- 1 cup water
- 1/3 cup milk (whole)
- 1.5 tsp tea
- 1.5 - 2 tsp sugar
- 1 pod cardamom (crushed)
- 1 clove (crushed)
- (?) some grated nutmeg

## Instructions

1. Boil water and milk together.
2. Add everything else.
3. Simmer for atleast 5 minutes and total volume is reduced to 1 cup
