---
title: Ikebana
date: 2024-09-24
category: poems
---

I bought flower frogs online  
Hoping to use this old glass vase of mine  
Ikebana, arranging flowers, I don't know any better  
Guess I'll go and buy some from the store nearby

The lady directs my gaze towards a crooked branch  
It's beautiful in it's own odd sort of way  
There's a rhythm, and I say yes immediately  
Billy balls catch my eye next, finishing up with some tiny dahlias

The vase won't do, I repurpose a japanese coffee mug that I never use  
It comes together effortlessly, minor tweaking  
Noone told me that arranging flowers  
Would make me burst into tears
