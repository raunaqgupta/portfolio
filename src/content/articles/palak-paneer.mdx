>> servings: 2
>> time: 30 minutes

Blanch 8oz of @spinach{8%oz}, 2oz of @kale{2%oz}, and 2oz of @fenugreek leaves{2%oz} in boiling water for 2 minutes.
Add to blender alongwith 6 whole @green chilies{6}, and 3 @garlic cloves{3} and blend to a smooth puree.
Cube 200g of @paneer{200%g} into bite-sized pieces.
Heat 1 tbsp of @ghee{1%tbsp} in a pan. Add 1 tsp of @cumin seeds{1%tsp}, 1 tsp of @mustard seeds{1%tsp} and let them splutter.
Add the puree to the pan and mix well. Cook for 5-7 minutes on medium heat.
Add the paneer cubes and simmer for another 5 minutes.
