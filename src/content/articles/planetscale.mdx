## Creating a cloud database from scratch

PlanetScale spun up from Vitess, an open-source database clustering system. The question was how do we deliver sharding as a plug-and-play solution to developers?

## Designing for speed

I was the sole product designer leading all design efforts while supporting an engineering team that grew from 10 to 30 engineers. I also contributed to frontend efforts and maintenance of user-facing surfaces such as the marketing site, documentation site, and the web application itself.

## Impact

PlanetScale raised a Series B round of $20 million led by Andreessen Horowitz in October 2020. This was within 1.5 years of the company's inception.
