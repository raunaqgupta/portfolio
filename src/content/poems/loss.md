title: Loss
date: 2024-04-30
---

There's loss and then there's loss  
I once lost my wallet on a train on my way to Delhi  
I lost my passport in a cab on my way to the airport  
I lost my grandmom who once told me to organize my toys better  
I lost my teenage years to yearnings and expectations  
I lost my childhood to my mother  
Can I lose you? You were never mine.
