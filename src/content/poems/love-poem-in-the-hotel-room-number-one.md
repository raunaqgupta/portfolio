---
title: Love poem in the hotel room number one
date: 2024-04-28
---

As I trace the shape of your eyebrows with my fingertips  
As your forehead calls me out to press my lips to it  
As I stand next to you at the red light hoping it doesn't turn green  
As the weekend decides to run out and I have to figure out the time of my departure  
As the sun sets and wind blows cold and the blanket beckons to be folded
