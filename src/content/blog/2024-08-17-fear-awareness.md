title: Fear awareness
date: 2024-08-17
---

One of the interesting situations to come out of being aware, and categorizing / naming fear as a underlying motivational factor while making decisions is this: What happens when two opposing decisions in a situation are both fear driven? It's either an incorrect categorization which is possible since the skill of naming fear is releatively new, or it's the classic between a rock and a hard place situation. None of these are unique human situations and so, either the skill has to be honed or the only way out is something akin to BATNA.
