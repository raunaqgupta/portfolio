title: A structured context layer for modeling product and design decisions
date: 2026-11-18

I've been refining a set of skills that collect concepts from [jobs-to-be-done](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jobs_to_be_done), [domain-driven design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain-driven_design), and ontologies to build out a structured context layer to help with modeling product and design decisions. The skills create and refine six kinds of interlinked notes:

- **Entities**: the core nouns of a product, with their fields, relations, and invariants.
- **Jobs**: the jobs users hire the product to do, including the situation, the outcome, and the forces acting on them.
- **Performers**: the roles that execute those jobs, defined by what they do rather than who they are.
- **Surfaces**: where the product is delivered, such as a web app, an API, or an MCP server.
- **Workflows**: the concrete sequences of steps that carry out a job.
- **Constraints**: implementation commitments, kept separate from the functional notes they apply to.

Notes link to each other using wiki links, so the same context can also be opened as an [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) vault and browsed through the graph view.
