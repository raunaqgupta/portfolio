---
title: My favorite mass market candy
date: 2024-04-17
---

The one candy that has always appealed to me is the Kinder Bueno. It's milk chocolate encased in wafer; textures that have always been my favorite. I also always taste an undertone of cardamom which is nowhere to be found in the ingredients or on sub-reddits which leads me to believe that this is a self-imposed perception.
