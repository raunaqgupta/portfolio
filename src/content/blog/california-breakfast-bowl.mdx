>> servings: 1
>> time: 5 minutes

Fry 2 @eggs{2} sunny side up.
As the eggs are cooking, scoop out 1 @avocado{1} into a bowl.
Add 4 tablespoons of @greek yogurt{4%tbsp} and mash together.
Season with @salt{to taste}, @pepper{to taste}, and a teaspoon of @turmeric{1%tsp}.
Optionally, add 2 tablespoons of @kimchi{2%tbsp}.
Once the eggs are done, place them on top of the avocado mix.
Top up with a handful of @potato chips{1%handful}.
