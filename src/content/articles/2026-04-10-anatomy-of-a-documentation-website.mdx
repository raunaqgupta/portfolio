Documentation websites are perfect artifacts of web design with some very well-defined constraints.Within these constraints, they provide lots of wiggle room for experimenting with typography, spacing, color, layout, and component design.

I'm fascinated by the all the variations that various documentation websites employ. This article is an attempt to understand why certain decisions look nicer than others, and synthesize the research that I've done on this topic.

## Variations

Most documentation websites tend to have the following core components that make up the layout:

- Header
- Sidebar
- Content
- Table of contents
- Footer
