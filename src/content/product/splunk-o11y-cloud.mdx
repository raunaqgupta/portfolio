## Overview

Splunk's acquisition replaced Cisco's existing efforts in Observability. Splunk Observability Cloud provides application performance monitoring, and infrastructure monitoring for cloud-native environments.

## Addressing the gap and expanding capabilities

![Extending capabilities at the intersection of applications and infrastructure](https://r2-image.raunaqgupta.workers.dev/dbmon-for-splunk-o11y-cloud/problem.png?f=avif)

As part of my work on Splunk Observability Cloud, I led the design efforts for two new verticals: Database Monitoring and Application Optimization. These additions aimed to enhance the platform's capabilities and provide users with a more comprehensive observability solution.

I also contributed new design patterns, components, and visualizations to Splunk's design system.

## Designing for scalability

I supported two product and engineering teams each working independently within their respective domains by delivering designs that could scale across these new verticals while supporting future extensions. This included:

1. A new information architecture proposal,
2. Defining a taxonomy to establish new UI patterns,
3. Defining product strategy for releasing new beta features within the product,
4. In-product marketing to enable product-led adoption, and
5. Paving the way for future AI-assisted workflows.

## Impact

Alpha launch of Database monitoring had 11 multinational enterprise customers with a conversion rate of 64% within 3 months of launch. This number is currently estimated to grow to 25 by the end of Q1 2026.
