---
title: From why to what
date: 2024-08-17
---

I used to cry, infant-like bawling  
Asking this ephemeral entity that supposedly puppets me  
Why is this or that happening to me?  
Powerless, pathetic

I cry still, hot tears, incisive awareness  
Asking still to an ephemeral entity  
What is the reason for this content of experience  
Curious, powerful
