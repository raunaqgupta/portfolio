>> servings: 1
>> time: 10 minutes

Boil 1/4 cup of @water{1/4%cup}.
Steep 1 teabag of strong @black tea{1%teabag} for 5 minutes.
Add a heaping teaspoon of @dandy blend{1%teaspoon}. The dandy blend contains both dandelion and chicory.
Add a teaspoon of @honey{1%teaspoon} to sweeten. Adjust to taste.
Add 3/4 cup of @whole milk{3/4%cup}.
Microwave for 1 minute on high and serve.
