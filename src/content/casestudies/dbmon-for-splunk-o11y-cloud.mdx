## Context

### Observability and Database Monitoring

Observability is a crucial aspect for ensuring reliability, performance, and user satisfaction of modern applications. Databases are the backbone of all business applications. To have a comprehensive view of a system, it is essential to monitor the databases that power these applications.

Splunk Observability Cloud is a Gartner leader in observability solutions, and this case study focuses on enhancing its database monitoring capabilities.

### Who benefits from Database monitoring?

Database monitoring fits into existing workflows for DevOps, SRE, and Database Administrators (DBAs). These professionals are responsible for ensuring the performance, reliability, and availability of applications and their underlying databases.

### Why build this?

Splunk Observability Cloud provided surface-level visibility into the performance of database queries impacting monitored applications. However, to truly understand database bottlenecks, it becamse apparent that deeper insights into the database server and its components were necessary.

Every competitor and other observability platforms had database monitoring capabilities. Customers were asking for it, and it was a glaring omission in Splunk Observability Cloud's feature set.

We also knew from Appdynamics's success that customers were willing to pay ~$50 million in ARR to . Therefore, we set out to build a robust database monitoring solution within Splunk Observability Cloud.
